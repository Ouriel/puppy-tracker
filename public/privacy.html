PupPace privacy policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

1. Information we collect

PupPace ("we", "our", or "us") collects essential information required to provide puppy activity tracking, feeding management, and predictions. This includes:

  • Account details: Your name and email address, from your Google Account when you sign in, and the household you belong to.
  • Dog profiles: Name, breed, sex, birth date, expected adult weight, meal goals, and health passport (vaccines, antiparasitics).
  • Activities: Timed entries of potty events (pee/poop, stool consistency, location), meals, weigh-ins, and treatments, with optional notes.
  • Visits: Anonymous page views and loading times, measured by Vercel without cookies.

2. How we use your information

Your data is used solely to provide platform functionality:

  • Predicting the next potty break and meal from your puppy's own history.
  • Sharing logs between the members of your household.
  • Generating a printable health summary for your veterinarian.
  • Checking that the app loads fast and which screens are used.

3. Data protection and storage

Sign-in uses signed tokens (JWT) and every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, pet activity logs, or contact details to any third parties.

4. Google sign-in data

When you sign in with Google, PupPace receives your basic Google profile and keeps only your name and email address, to identify you. We do not access, read, or store any other data from your Google Account.

5. Data deletion and questions

You may request the deletion of your account and all its data at any time. For questions regarding this policy, contact your household's admin, or write to matthieu.jacquet@gmail.com.
